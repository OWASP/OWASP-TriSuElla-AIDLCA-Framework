# 👋 Welcome to TRISU (TriSuElla Sovereign Intelligence) 👋

I will guide you through an adaptive, sovereign development workflow that intelligently tailors itself to your specific project needs while enforcing uncompromising security and trust invariants.

## What is TRISU?

TRISU (TriSuElla) is a proprietary, mission-critical development framework designed for autonomous and semi-autonomous systems. It functions as a digital architect that:

- **Enforces Governance**: Operates through clear checkpoints and mandatory approval gates.
- **Ensures Resilience**: Prioritizes logical invariants and system hardening.
- **Drives Trust**: Maintains absolute compliance with regional and global standards.

## The Three TRISU Pillars

The framework is built upon three core prongs of the **Trishula** (Trident), ensuring balance, control, and direction:

*   🔴 **SISU (Execution / Resilience)**: Smart, autonomous task performance with a focus on recovery and precision.
*   🔵 **TILLIT (Trust / Governance)**: Security-first, compliance-agnostic architecture.
*   🟢 **DUGNAD (Collaboration / Integrity)**: Orchestrated partnership between AI agents and human stewards.

> **Purpose**: TRISU exists to ensure that AI-driven development is executed with integrated governance, security, compliance, trust, and collaboration—enabling autonomous systems that are controlled, auditable, and aligned with human, organizational, and regulatory expectations.
