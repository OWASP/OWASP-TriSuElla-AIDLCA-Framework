# Extension: Core Enterprise GRC (SOC 2 Type II & ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS)
**Prefix**: `TRISU-SOC2`, `TRISU-ISMS` | **Rules**: 8 | **Scope**: Enterprise SaaS, SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001:2022

## When to Load This Extension
Load this extension if your application or AI workload is deployed in enterprise B2B, SaaS, or regulated environments requiring SOC 2 Type II attestation (CC6.1–CC8.1) or ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS) compliance.

## Key Controls
- **TRISU-SOC2-01 [CRITICAL]**: CC6.1 Logical Access Controls & MFA Enforcement.
- **TRISU-SOC2-02 [HIGH]**: CC6.6 & CC6.7 Data Protection & Boundary Encryption.
- **TRISU-SOC2-03 [CRITICAL]**: CC7.2 & CC7.3 Continuous Security Event Logging & Automated Anomaly Detection.
- **TRISU-SOC2-04 [CRITICAL]**: CC8.1 Strict Change Management & Automated CI/CD Gate Verification.
- **TRISU-ISMS-01 [HIGH]**: ISO/IEC 27001 Control A.5 Information Security Policies & Governance Review.
- **TRISU-ISMS-02 [HIGH]**: ISO/IEC 27001 Control A.6 Threat Intelligence & Segregation of Duties.
- **TRISU-ISMS-03 [HIGH]**: ISO/IEC 27001 Control A.7 Human Resources & Security Awareness Attestation.
- **TRISU-ISMS-04 [CRITICAL]**: ISO/IEC 27001 Control A.8 Secure Development Lifecycle & Vulnerability Remediation SLAs.

Full rules in `compliance-soc2-iso27001.md`.
