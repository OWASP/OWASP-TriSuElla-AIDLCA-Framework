# Extension: AI Risk Management (NIST AI RMF 1.0 & GenAI Profile)
**Prefix**: `TRISU-AIRMF` | **Rules**: 6 | **Scope**: AI/LLM Governance, NIST AI RMF 1.0, NIST.IR.8596

## When to Load This Extension
Load this extension if your project builds, fine-tunes, orchestrates, or integrates Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), or autonomous agents requiring formal AI risk management across the GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE functions.

## Key Controls
- **TRISU-AIRMF-01 [CRITICAL]**: GOVERN Function — Transparent AI Risk Governance & Accountable Roles.
- **TRISU-AIRMF-02 [HIGH]**: MAP Function — Context Categorization, AI System Profiling & Impact Assessment.
- **TRISU-AIRMF-03 [CRITICAL]**: MEASURE Function — Quantitative AI Risk Metrics, Bias & Hallucination Benchmarking.
- **TRISU-AIRMF-04 [CRITICAL]**: MANAGE Function — Continuous Risk Treatment, Fallbacks & AI Circuit Breakers.
- **TRISU-AIRMF-05 [HIGH]**: Generative AI Profile (NIST.IR.8596) — Synthetic Output Attestation & Provenance Watermarking.
- **TRISU-AIRMF-06 [CRITICAL]**: Continuous AI Red-Teaming & Dynamic Adversarial Stress Testing.
