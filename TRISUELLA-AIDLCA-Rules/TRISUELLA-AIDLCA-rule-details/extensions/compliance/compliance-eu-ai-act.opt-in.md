# Extension: EU AI Act High-Risk Compliance
**Prefix**: `TRISU-EUAI` | **Rules**: 7 | **Scope**: High-Risk AI, EU Market, CE Conformity

## When to Load This Extension
Load this extension if your AI application is deployed in the European Union or targets EU residents, particularly in high-risk categories under Annex III (recruitment, banking/credit scoring, healthcare, education, law enforcement, critical infrastructure).

## Key Controls
- **TRISU-EUAI-01 [CRITICAL]**: High-Risk Classification & CE-Conformity Gate (Art. 6 & Art. 43).
- **TRISU-EUAI-02 [HIGH]**: Continuous Risk Management System (Art. 9).
- **TRISU-EUAI-03 [CRITICAL]**: Data & Data Governance Integrity (Art. 10).
- **TRISU-EUAI-04 [HIGH]**: Technical Documentation & Architecture Dossier (Art. 11).
- **TRISU-EUAI-05 [HIGH]**: Automatic Event Logging & Record-Keeping (Art. 12).
- **TRISU-EUAI-06 [HIGH]**: Transparency & Deployer Information Disclosures (Art. 13).
- **TRISU-EUAI-07 [CRITICAL]**: Human Oversight & Override Architecture (Art. 14).

Full rules in `compliance-eu-ai-act.md`.
