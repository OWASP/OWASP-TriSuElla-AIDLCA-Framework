# FREE-AI Committee Report: Framework for Responsible and Ethical Enablement of AI
**August 2025 | Reserve Bank of India**

---

## Executive Summary
*AI is a transformative general-purpose technology. In the financial sector, it unlocks new customer engagement and efficiency but introduces risks like bias, lack of explainability, and cybersecurity vulnerabilities. The FREE-AI Committee formulated **7 Sutras** and **26 Recommendations** under **6 strategic Pillars**.*

---

## ☸️ The 7 Sutras (Guiding Principles)
1.  **Trust is the Foundation**: Trust is non-negotiable and should remain uncompromised.
2.  **People First**: AI should augment human decision-making but defer to human judgment and citizen interest.
3.  **Innovation over Restraint**: Foster responsible innovation with purpose.
4.  **Fairness and Equity**: AI outcomes should be fair and non-discriminatory.
5.  **Accountability**: Accountability rests with the entities deploying AI.
6.  **Understandable by Design**: Ensure explainability for trust.
7.  **Safety, Resilience, and Sustainability**: AI systems should be secure, resilient, and energy efficient.

---

## 🏛️ The 6 Strategic Pillars
### Innovation Enablement
1.  **Infrastructure**: Financial Sector Data Infrastructure, AI Innovation Sandbox.
2.  **Policy**: Adaptive and Enabling Policies, AI Liability Framework.
3.  **Capacity**: Capacity Building within REs, Skill development for Regulators.

### Risk Mitigation
4.  **Governance**: Board-Approved AI Policy, Data Lifecycle Governance, AI System Governance.
5.  **Protection**: Consumer Protection, Cybersecurity Measures, Red-Teaming, BCP for AI.
6.  **Assurance**: AI Inventory, AI Audit Framework, Public Disclosures.

---

## 📋 Key Recommendations (Rules Context)

### Governance [RULE-01]
*   **Board-Approved AI Policy**: REs must establish a policy covering governance structure, accountability, risk appetite, and AI disclosures.
*   **Risk Classification**: Categorize AI use cases into **Low, Medium, and High risk**. High-risk involves critical functions like credit underwriting or autonomous fund movement.

### Data & Model Lifecycle [RULE-02]
*   **Data Lifecycle Governance**: Internal controls for collection, access, usage, retention, and deletion compliant with DPDP Act.
*   **Model Inventory**: Semi-annual update of all AI models, algorithms, use cases, and dependencies.

### Cybersecurity & Protection [RULE-03]
*   **Cybersecurity Measures**: Strengthen ecosystems (hardware, software, processes) to address AI-specific risks like model poisoning and adversarial attacks.
*   **Red-Teaming**: Regular (at least semi-annual) structured adversarial testing for High/Medium risk applications.
*   **Business Continuity (BCP)**: Include AI model-specific performance degradation and fallback mechanisms (mandatory human review on drift).

### Consumer & Ethics [RULE-04]
*   **Explainability**: Decision logic must be explainable and fair. Users must be informed when interacting with AI.
*   **Grievance Redressal**: Provide customers means to challenge and seek clarification on AI decisions.

### Accountability & Audit [RULE-05]
*   **AI Audit Framework**: Risk-based audit covering data inputs, model architecture, and decision outputs.
*   **Public Disclosures**: AI usage and safeguards must be part of annual reports and websites.

## 🔐 Glossary of Key Terms (Extract)
*   **Agentic AI**: Automated entity that senses, responds to environment, and takes actions to achieve goals (ISO/IEC 22989).
*   **Model-on-Model Risk**: Risk arising when one AI system oversees another and fails, causing cascading errors.
*   **A2A Protocol**: Communication protocol enabling autonomous agents to interact without human involvement.
*   **Mosaic Attack**: Aggregating innocuous data points to reveal sensitive information.
*   **Model Inversion**: Reverse-engineering models to extract training data.
